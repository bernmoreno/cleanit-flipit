Our Story

Built on Trust,

Clean It Flip It was founded with a simple mission: to give homeowners and real estate investors a cleaning partner they can genuinely trust. We started as a small local team and have grown into a full-service cleaning and property transformation company serving hundreds of satisfied clients.

Whether you're a homeowner who deserves a spotless space, a landlord preparing for new tenants, or a house flipper who needs a fast turnaround — we have the team, experience, and work ethic to get the job done right.
